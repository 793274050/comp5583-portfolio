My contribution ↗

Goal: Answer questions about driving, faults, maintenance, and insurance using uploaded vehicle documents.

My contribution ↗

I prepared automotive instruction data, built a multi-turn RAG application grounded in vehicle manuals and insurance documents, and adapted Qwen with LoRA. I compared model answers before and after fine-tuning to inspect accuracy and consistency.

Any benchmark figures from this internship are preliminary drafts and are omitted here until verified.

Qwen · LoRA · Automotive RAG
