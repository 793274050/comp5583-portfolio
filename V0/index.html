01AI AGENTS

Multi-Agent Business Travel Assistant

Goal: Make business travel planning and policy questions easier to handle through coordinated agents.

My contribution ↗

I developed a multi-agent workflow that divides travel requests into focused tasks and brings the results together for the user. I worked on orchestration and document retrieval so answers could refer to travel information instead of relying only on the model's memory.

Agent orchestration · Retrieval · Travel workflow

02HUAWEI INTERNSHIP

Technical Q&A with Qwen

Goal: Improve technical question answering by combining an open-source Qwen model with document retrieval and domain adaptation.

My contribution ↗

I participated in preparing domain question-answer data, building a RAG-based document workflow, and applying LoRA fine-tuning to Qwen. I compared answers before and after adaptation to identify where domain accuracy and response consistency could improve.

Any benchmark figures from this internship are preliminary drafts and are omitted here until verified.

Qwen · LoRA · RAG

My contribution ↗

Risk modelling · Feature analysis · Data
